We compared six OpenRouter services on 4,785 shared quality cases, with chat reasoning disabled and the same compact probability-of-yes output. Jev was fastest across all five timing tasks, with task medians of 0.254–0.267 seconds; DeepSeek V4.1 Flash was the fastest chat model at 0.792–0.944 seconds. Quality depended on the task: Jev had the highest observed BoolQ, RTE and simple-policy accuracy, DeepSeek had the highest WiC score, and Luna had the lowest exact-probability error. Luna cost more than Jev on the short binary workloads. With verified long-prefix caching, Luna cost 35% less than Jev, including its four cold cache primes, but scored 86.46% versus Jev’s 96.88%. The result supports task-specific choices: Luna’s cost advantage depends on input reuse, and none of the tested chat alternatives matched Jev’s response speed.
